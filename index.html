  4. CWL
    Cardiff · Aug 2025 onwards

    The AI programme at Innovi

    Back in Cardiff for final year, I took on the AI programme at Innovi Advisors, a UK accountancy practice, part-time. I sat with the partners to find where staff time went, then turned 15 stalled draft agents into one agent the team now uses day to day. I run the staff training too.

  5. 04
    First client · Summer 2026

    A national pharmacy group

    This summer I got my first client of my own, a national UK pharmacy group with about £500m turnover, delivered through Innovi. I spent three days on site with Finance and HR, and three Copilot agents shipped. HR staff built two of them after an hour with me. Then I wrote the Executive Director a letter on what worked and who should own the next phase.
